You buy a $20, $50, or $250 card. The recipient gets an email with a
code, redeems it at boon.gifts, taps through an OTP, and starts asking
GPT-5, Claude, Gemini, or whoever else they want questions about their
kid's homework, the next product idea, or the weird rash on their arm.
No subscription. No account. No relationship to maintain. When the
balance hits zero, the card's done.

The audience is everyone in your life, but in particular the people who
would never go set up their own ChatGPT or Claude account on their own.

## Build

Built in one day, including learning Stripe, Resend, and OpenRouter
from scratch. Stripe went into live mode the same evening, and the full
path (purchase, webhook, email delivery, redemption, OTP, runtime key
issue, inference) runs against real cards.

The interesting engineering wasn't the SDKs, which are all
well-documented. It was the trust architecture: how do you make a
transferable claim on metered third-party API spend without the
purchaser, the recipient, or the platform getting burned?

The answer was a vendor-provisioning API as ledger. Each card is a row
with a provider-side capability key, gated behind a recipient-bound
OTP. The site never holds long-lived model credentials in the browser,
and the recipient spends without ever creating an account.

## Stack

FastAPI and Postgres in Docker on Railway. Resend for email, with DKIM
and SPF verified. Stripe in live mode. OpenRouter for the model layer.
Domain on Cloudflare.
