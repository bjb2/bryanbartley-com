You land on a question instead of a sales pitch: where do you actually
fit as AI moves through the economy? Eight mastery-gated layers take you
from reasoning structurally about automation, through a task-level audit
of your own job, to a dated 12-month plan with kill criteria and a review
cadence. Layers unlock at 80% mastery of the previous one, because moving
on at 60% understanding compounds into being permanently behind on every
later prerequisite.

The audience is the mid-career professional who feels the ground shifting
and wants a method, not motivational noise. People who would rather audit
their own exposure task by task than doom-scroll capability headlines.

## The method

Structural reasoning over forecasts. Work gets scored on four axes,
structure, embodiment, accountability, and stakes, drawn from the
labor-economics literature: Autor, Acemoglu and Restrepo, Eloundou's
task-level exposure study, Ericsson on deliberate practice, and Dunlosky
on which study techniques actually work. Every lesson follows an
evidence-based template: a cold retrieval prompt, a concrete instance, an
interactive widget, the abstraction, a worked example, and a
retrieval-framed recap. Free primary sources throughout, each link
verified at authoring time.

## Pathways

A Pathways explorer pulls real occupations and their task lists from the
U.S. Department of Labor's O\*NET database, bundled at build time under a
CC BY 4.0 license, and runs each occupation through the same four-axis
durability read the curriculum teaches. You can import an occupation's
actual tasks straight into your exposure map and start the audit from real
data instead of a blank page.

## The learning engine

Forty-two lessons across eight layers, with four exercise kinds including
forced primary-source reading. Completed exercises feed an SM-2
spaced-repetition queue. The four artifacts the curriculum produces, an
exposure map, a candidate shortlist, a 12-month plan, and an annual
re-audit, live in a structured workbook with markdown export. Progress and
workbook both persist in the browser; nothing leaves the device, and a
one-file backup guards against a cleared cache.

## Stack

Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, MDX. Content is a graph of MDX lessons
plus JSON exercise packs, gated by a content linter that checks the
manifest, exercise validity, and source links. The O\*NET occupation data
is ingested by a build-time script. Open-source under MIT, deployed to
GitHub Pages.
