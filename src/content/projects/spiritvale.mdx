SpiritVale is an indie MMORPG on Steam. I started on the outside, building
[SpiritValers](/work/spiritvalers), its community wiki. That got me into the
game's repository. Now I design UI for the client and build it myself.

My first commit landed on November 3. Patch 0.35.3 went out the next day with
five of my changes in its notes:

- a new login screen
- a full loot filter on its own Settings page
- a volume slider for loot sounds, so you can mute everything except drops
- a toggle for auto-attacking after a skill
- a support link in Settings

## The loot filter

Players decide which drops get picked up by their loot key and their pet. They
also decide how a drop looks and sounds on the ground. A drop-rate threshold
covers most of it. Any category can override that threshold to collect
everything or to leave drops for manual pickup. Equipment and artifacts can also
keep only drops with a minimum number of max-value stats.

Personal rules sit on top of all that. The first match wins. A rule can target a
whole category or narrow down to one of roughly 850 items. It can also test the
rolled stats, like crit damage at least +7% or MP cost at most -10%. Values come
from a dropdown of what the stat can actually roll, the way the auction house
filter works, so nobody types a threshold that can never hit. A matching rule
can force the pickup and set the drop's line color and sound.

The page previews ground loot with the same evaluator the server runs. On the
server, every drop is checked against each player's own filter. That includes
parties on Even Share, where a member whose filter rejects a drop gets skipped.
Rules never hide anything: click a drop and it's yours.

## The login screen

The old login screen was a region table next to a wall of patch-note text. Now a
side rail shows each region's ping and how many players are online. Behind it,
the key art crossfades every seven seconds. The latest three Steam announcements
sit as cards, each wearing the art from its own Steam post, and open into a full
panel. Every new string ships in all 17 of the game's languages.
