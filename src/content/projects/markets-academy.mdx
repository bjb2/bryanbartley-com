Eleven layers from how money actually works down through asset pricing,
options theory, and market microstructure. Every lesson follows a
deliberate template: retrieval prompt, concrete example, interactive
widget or video, abstraction, worked example, recap. Exercises range
from multiple-choice through real Python that runs in the browser via
Pyodide.

The audience is serious self-learners who don't want a finance book
but also don't want fluff. Personal investors who've outgrown the
basics. Software engineers crossing into fintech. Students whose
finance class skipped the mechanism.

## The spine

Yale's Shiller lectures. MIT 15.401, 15.402, and 15.433. Columbia's
Mehrling on the money view. Khan Academy. Primary texts from Hayek,
Bernstein, Bogle, Buffett, Marks. The CBOE Options Institute. Joel
Hasbrouck on microstructure. Free sources, working scholars, no
re-sold content.

## Status

Layers 0 and 1 are shipped with the full Khan-template treatment.
Layers 2 through 10 are stubbed out, waiting on writing. Six
interactive SVG widgets so far: compounding, yield curve, money
hierarchy, supply and demand, Fisher calculator, Rule of 72.
Open-source under MIT, deployed to GitHub Pages.
