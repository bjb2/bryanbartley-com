You install systema-claude, drop your writing, projects, and a short
voice document into the seed folders, and open the app. Any AI agent
session you start in that workspace orients to who you are in under a
minute. Your active projects, your principles, the way you actually
talk. When that session ends and a new one starts cold, it gets the
same orientation.

The audience is anyone who works with Claude, Codex, or Copilot day
to day and is tired of explaining themselves at the start of every
session.

## Why this works

Continuity lives in the architecture, not in any agent's memory.
Flat markdown files with structured frontmatter are the substrate.
A Rust daemon (orgd) handles indexing, file-watching, and the HTTP
and IPC routes the viewer talks to. The Tauri shell renders the
documents, edits them, and shows the graph of how they connect.

An agent reading CLAUDE.md plus three short context documents is
oriented to your work before its first response. Without that
scaffolding it either guesses or starts from a corporate-default
tone neither of you wants.
