Outgoing is a curated local-events newsletter operating across NYC,
the Bay Area, and Ann Arbor. The engagement covers paid acquisition
on Meta and Google, organic social production, partnership outreach,
and the creative engine that produces all of it.

## What got built

A Remotion-based programmatic video pipeline (`outgoing-creative-engine`)
that produces vertical and square ad creative on demand. Captions are
hand-curated from faster-whisper word-level transcripts; motion design
follows a distilled rulebook of seven hard rules and eight archetype
templates pulled from a deep research pass on what's actually working
on Meta in 2026.

A second pipeline (`meta-mirror`) is a single-tenant Python and DuckDB
data layer over Meta's Marketing API. It surfaces per-variant
performance and pixel-health signals that Ads Manager buries. That's
the difference between catching a tracking blackout in twelve hours
versus three weeks.

When stock libraries couldn't honor a brief (NYC venue specificity
in editorial flat-lay photography), the creative pipeline added AI
image generation through Gemini 3 Pro with real venue names baked
into the prompt.

## Why it matters as a portfolio piece

Most growth marketing is hearsay. Treating it as a data
infrastructure problem — capture primary data, validate attribution
at the source, catch pixel failures the same day — consistently
outperforms playbook execution. That's the unlock the engagement is
built around.
