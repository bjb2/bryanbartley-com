You open the map. Pins cluster around award winners across the country.
Click one and you get the restaurant, its awards, what to know going in.
Filter by James Beard year, Michelin tier, or cuisine. Save to a list.
Plan a trip around it.

The audience is people who eat out seriously. Travelers planning where
to spend an anniversary dinner. The friend everyone asks "where should
I eat in Houston?" The category is over-served by Yelp aggregate
ratings and under-served by anything actually opinionated.

## Why it works the way it does

A restaurant earns a slot only if a domain expert in that cuisine
praises it. Awards from James Beard, Michelin, regional critics, the
World's 50 Best. Not "popular" places, not "trending" places. The
guide trades the long tail of decent restaurants for a short list
worth crossing a city for.

## Stack

Static JSON pre-built offline; no runtime API calls for the map data.
The whole thing loads without any third-party service running, and the
build is reproducible from primary place IDs. Hosted on Vercel,
Supabase behind the editorial back-end.
